# Surface brief: CA Nishita Gandhi multipage site

Scope: whole site (home + 29 internal routes). Mode: Persuade on home; Read on service, Knowledge Centre and legal pages.
Audience/job: salaried individuals first (ITR, capital gains, notices), then NRIs and businesses. Action: call, email, or send the enquiry form (mailto handoff).
Constraints: ICAI no-solicitation; copy ported verbatim from live site; navy/cream/teal pinned; restrained motion; reduced motion; fast load; no invented claims.

## Direction contract
THESIS: "Your whole file." The practice keeps one consistent file across income tax, GST and company law. The site is that file: an indexed, ledger-like set of pages, not a stack of marketing cards. Refuses the category default of icon-card grids and stock CA imagery.
OWN-WORLD: navy ground (#0E1A2B) for page heads and closes, cream paper (#F6F3EC) for reading, teal (#5EC4B6 / #1A6B62) for action, state, and the single hero emphasis ("handled.", carried from the client-approved incumbent). Caslon display (statute and ledger printing) over Manrope text. Hairline rules, ruled ledger rows, tabular numerals, folder-tab details. No eyebrows, no section numbers, no icon-tile grids.
STORY: visitor sees a real CA and a real file, finds their situation in the index, reads exactly what's covered and what documents to keep ready, then calls or writes.
FIRST VIEWPORT: navy hero; left, H1 at display scale with the keyword subhead, primary "Get in touch" and a secondary path for income tax returns; right, a WebGL file stack (sheets ITR, Form 16, AIS, GSTR-3B, Form 3CD, each named on a divider tab, settle into a slate folder labelled "Your file"). No portrait or lede in the hero (client request). Still image (same pose) on phones and reduced motion; on phones the stage sits between the headline and the CTAs.
FORM: extension of the incumbent, brief-pinned world; no concept roll (established world). Signature interaction: the file stack assembling on load and tilting with pointer and scroll. Motion grammar: CSS page crossfade, one-time scroll reveals from visible-by-default content, hover 150-250ms.
FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance
