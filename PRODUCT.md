# Product

## Platform

web

## Users
Primary: salaried individuals in and around Mulund, Mumbai, who need their income tax return filed correctly, capital gains (flat sale, redevelopment, shares) handled, or an income tax notice answered. They arrive unsure what they need and want a qualified person to take ownership.
Also served (confirmed by the live site, secondary on this build): NRIs, professionals, proprietors and family businesses, private companies and LLPs, contractors, businesses dealing with foreign parties, startups.

## Product Purpose
The website of CA Nishita Gandhi, a practising Chartered Accountant (ICAI, 2015) with her own practice in Mulund West. It explains what the practice handles, for whom, and how, so a visitor can decide to call, email or send an enquiry. Success: a visitor understands which service fits their situation and makes contact.

## Positioning
One Chartered Accountant personally reviews and signs off every engagement; the person you talk to is the person signing. Ten years as a partner in practice before setting up her own. Works in English, Hindi and Gujarati, in person by appointment in Mulund or fully online.

## Operating Context
Visitors read on phones as often as desktops, often after receiving a notice or before a due date. Work runs on documents (Form 16, AIS/26AS, bank statements, GST returns, books of account), government portals (Income Tax e-filing, GST, MCA, TRACES), and statutory due dates.

## Capabilities and Constraints
- 15 services in six groups: Income Tax (ITR & tax planning, corporate tax, notices & appeals, TDS/TCS, NRI & international), GST (registration & returns; advisory, notices & refunds), Audit & Assurance (statutory, tax, internal & stock), Business & Corporate (setup & registrations, ROC/MCA), Accounting & Advisory (accounting/finalisation/payroll, project finance & CMA), Certifications (CA certificates).
- Site structure mirrors the live site: home, about, services index + 15 service pages, who we work with, Knowledge Centre (updates, compliance calendar, FAQs, useful links), Mulund local page, contact, disclaimer, privacy policy, terms of use, sitemap.
- No backend: the contact form hands off to the visitor's email app; it must never claim a message was sent.
- ICAI rules: chartered accountants may not solicit or advertise. Content is informational; no promotional claims, rankings, testimonials or fee promises.
- Stack: Next.js 14 (App Router), Tailwind, shadcn/ui on Radix, framer-motion.

## Product Principles
- The person signing is the person you speak to: every page leads back to Nishita directly.
- Plain language over jargon; name the form, the section and the due date when it helps.
- Inform, never solicit: facts, scope and process, no sales claims.
- Due dates are approximate and change; say so wherever dates appear.

## Accessibility & Inclusion
Readers include senior citizens: generous type sizes, strong contrast, keyboard access, prefers-reduced-motion honoured, tap-to-call everywhere.
